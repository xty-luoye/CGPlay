FFmpeg: distinct native-library and command-line builds
------------------------------------------------------
The inspected local Release avcodec-62.dll identifies LGPL version 2.1 or later.
Its vcpkg shared-build configuration disables libx264/libx265 and does not enable
GPL or nonfree options. The standalone ffmpeg.exe/ffprobe.exe in the existing
full package are a different Gyan 8.1.1 build, reported as GPLv3-or-later with
GPL features enabled. The application calls the standalone tools through
processes. These observations do not certify future builds or relicense FFmpeg.
See FFMPEG_SOURCE_OFFER.txt for provenance and outstanding source verification.
  https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
